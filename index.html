Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible to donate plasma, you must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Weigh at least 110 pounds and be in good health**
  • Have a permanent local address area of the facility where you would like to donate. The recruitment area varies by location.

*Based on your center location

**Health will be determined at your medical screening.
